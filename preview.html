How it works on the real board

  1. A New request button appears at the top of the board. Admins also see Form settings.
  2. The form validates required fields, scans every card on the board and takes the highest ID in the chosen option's block (100s, 200s, …) plus one, then creates the card in the configured list with the selector and type labels. The thumbnail becomes the card cover.
  3. Links are added as attachments and to the description. Pasted images are uploaded to the card and shown inline.
  4. Every word you see in the form (title, field labels, option names, hints) and every mapping to a label is edited in Settings and stored on the board itself. The code carries only the neutral placeholders shown in this demo.
